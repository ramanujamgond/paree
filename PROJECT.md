# Pyaree project reference

This repository contains the Pyaree application. This file consolidates the useful brand, client and implementation context from the former documentation directories. The original product photos live only in `public/images/`; additional website artwork lives in its `art/` subdirectory.

## Website

- Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, responsive authored CSS and Motion.
- Pages: home, products, seven product details, story, philosophy, process, FAQ and contact.
- Ordering: size and quantity selection, persistent order drawer, INR totals and a formatted WhatsApp message. Instagram is the fallback when WhatsApp configuration is missing.
- Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the production origin before deployment.
- Keep real packaging intact; never invent prices, sizes, discounts, ratings, certifications, health claims, delivery policies or missing business details.
- Coconut orders are 250 ml; the supplied photo shows older 200 ml packaging. Castor has no supplied description or photograph and belongs separately from the cooking oils.
- Reference direction: agricultural imagery, forest green, mustard accents, pale warm sections, image-led layouts, generous spacing and restrained motion. The reference illustrations depicted a farming app and are visual inspiration, not Pyaree business facts.
- Obsolete implementation plans, review iterations, duplicate photographs, original reference screenshots and the PDF duplicate were removed after retaining client data and the complete extracted brand narrative here.

Company Details

Barik Agro Industries is a proprietorship-based FMCG enterprise based in Balangir, Odisha,
India, focused on bringing quality-conscious food and consumer products to customers
through trusted brands.

The company’s flagship consumer brand is Pyaree Woodpressed Oils, offering a range of
traditionally wood-pressed edible oils with an emphasis on quality, responsible sourcing,
transparency and consumer trust.

Barik Agro Industries aims to gradually expand beyond edible oils into other carefully
selected FMCG and food categories, building a portfolio of consumer brands that combine
quality, thoughtful product selection and responsible communication.

Our Philosophy

Apno Ke Liye, Pyar Se.

Some of the deepest expressions of love happen quietly.
--- page 2 ---

In the food we cook.
In the ingredients we choose.
In the way we serve our family.
And in the little things we do every day for the people who matter most.

Pyaree was born from this feeling.

We believe that food is not just about filling a plate. It is about bringing people together,
creating memories, and making an ordinary meal feel a little more special.

That is why we bring you unrefined, unheated, 100% pure wood-pressed oils — oils that
retain their natural aroma and character, so that the food you cook can speak for itself.

We believe in moderation, too.

Oil is energy-dense. And because we care about the people who bring Pyaree into their
homes, we believe in using oil mindfully and in moderation.

We don’t want you to hand over the responsibility for your health to an oil brand.

Your health is yours. Your choices are yours.

Pyaree is not a medicine, and we don’t want to make promises that an oil cannot keep.

Our promise is much simpler — and much more honest.

We want to give your food the aroma and taste of a good wood-pressed oil, so you can
enjoy that richness with less oil.

Because when an oil brings enough aroma and flavour to your food, you may not need to
pour as much.

And perhaps, that is a small but meaningful way of caring for the people you love.

Not by telling them what will make them healthy.

But by giving them something good, and reminding them that how much we use matters
too.

That is why Pyaree exists.

For the food we make with our hands.
For the people we make it for.
For the love that goes into every meal.

The Story of Pyaree

A question from our own kitchen became a journey of two years.

Pyaree did not begin with a business plan.

It began with a simple question:

--- page 4 ---
Can we bring an oil to our kitchen that is closer to its natural form?

As we looked more closely at refined oils and how they are processed, we found ourselves
wanting something different. Refining can involve multiple processing steps, including
high-temperature processing and chemical processing aids.

We wanted to explore an alternative — an oil that could be brought to the table unrefined
and without intentional heating during pressing, while preserving the natural character of
the oilseed.

That question stayed with us.

And we decided to find the answer ourselves.

For the next two years, we researched, travelled and learned.

We visited different oil-processing facilities. We went to mandis in search of high-quality
oilseeds. We met people involved in sourcing and processing. We studied different seeds,
explored different methods and kept asking the same question:

Can we make an oil that we would be proud to serve to our own family?

We weren’t looking for the easiest way to make an oil.

We were looking for the right way to make one.

After two years of research, visits, conversations and countless learnings, that journey gave
birth to Pyaree Woodpressed Oils.
--- page 5 ---

Our commitment is simple:

Unrefined. Unheated. 100% Pure Wood-Pressed Oils.

We want to preserve the natural aroma, character and richness of carefully selected
oilseeds and bring that experience into everyday cooking.

For us, Pyaree is deeply personal.

Because before it became a brand, it was something we wanted for our own home.

And that is still the standard we try to hold ourselves to — would we be happy putting this
on our family’s table?

If the answer is yes, we know we are on the right path.
